From a search to your own tier list.

Catalogue search finds stories. Your reading history shapes tentative placements for the books you save.

Explore

Find a book

  1. 1

    Build the catalogue index

    Titles, aliases, authors, descriptions, and tags become searchable records. The local sentence encoder turns each record into a 384-number vector.

  2. 2

    Search by words or meaning

    The same encoder processes your query. FAISS finds nearby vectors while exact titles and matching metadata contribute keyword signals.

  3. 3

    Narrow the results

    Selected tags filter the ranked results. Guests see the catalogue preview; signing in opens the full collection.

Search model all-MiniLM-L6-v2 runs in this service. The search index is rebuilt when catalogue metadata changes.

02

Organize

Make the list yours

  1. 1

    Save what you read

    Reading status, ratings, and tier choices are stored in your account's library.

  2. 2

    Get a tentative tier

    Your ratings and direct tier placements form a weighted baseline. Books sharing tags with those choices draw on those signals; reading status adjusts their weight.

  3. 3

    Refine it by moving books

    Moving, rating, or confirming a book adds feedback. Your chosen tiers stay in place while tentative placements for saved books update.

Questions

How novelist works

How are tentative tiers chosen?

Direct placements and ratings establish your baseline. Shared tags and the title’s reading state adjust that estimate. Dropped titles start conservatively.

What teaches the ranking model?

Rating, dragging, assigning a tier, or confirming a suggestion records feedback. Later predictions use those choices, with more weight on completed and dropped titles.

Why might a cover be missing?

A source may not publish one, may block hotlinking, or may have moved the image. novelist tries its local cache, a safe proxy, and the source URL before showing a placeholder.

Can readers add novels?

Readers can request a title. Administrators can run exact-title discovery or broader collection jobs so the shared catalogue stays controlled and deduplicated.
